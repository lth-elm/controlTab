# controlTab privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

controlTab does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It has no server, makes no network
requests, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## What the extension handles, and where it stays

Everything below is handled inside your browser, on your computer.

- **Tab information.** The title, address and icon of the tabs you view during a browser session,
  so that they can be listed in the switcher.
- **Preview images.** A small picture of a page, taken when you view its tab, so that the switcher
  can show it.

Tab information and preview images are kept in the browser's session memory only. They are erased
when the browser closes, when a tab is closed, and when you clear the list (Cmd+Delete in the
switcher, or the "Clear recent tabs" button in the settings).

- **Your settings.** The options you choose in the settings (switcher or direct mode, and what the
  Delete key does) are saved in the browser's extension storage. If you use your browser's sync
  feature, the browser may copy these settings between your own devices.

None of this is ever sent to the developer or to any third party.

## Why the permissions are needed

- **Access to all sites:** to draw the switcher on top of the page you are on, to notice when you
  release the shortcut keys, and to take the preview images.
- **Tabs and favicon:** to read the titles and icons shown in the switcher.
- **Scripting:** to make the switcher available in tabs that were already open when the extension
  was installed.
- **Storage:** to keep your settings, and the recent list and previews for the session.

## Limited use

The use of information handled by controlTab complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy,
including the Limited Use requirements.
